About Imre Creative Studio

Imre Creative Studio is an independent Android app development studio based in Switzerland. We create original applications designed to solve real-world needs – from fishing and data-driven tools to AI-powered features, dream journaling and meditation.

We focus on reliable performance, clear structure, thoughtful design and long-term value. Our projects are continuously improved, refined and expanded. If you have a question or need support, you can contact us directly.
